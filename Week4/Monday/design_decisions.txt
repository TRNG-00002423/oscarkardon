I made 5 tables to contain all the data required for this use case. A customer can have multiple adresses which is why Customers 
-> Adresses is a one to many relationship. A customer can also have multiple orders so there is a one to many relationship with Customers to 
orders. Email must be unique in cutomers. SKU is also unique in Product. 
A direct many to many relationship between Orders and Product would not be able to contain information such as the quantity of the order and the 
price at the time of the order since price could change in the future.
